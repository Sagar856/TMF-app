Information used

MINARTH stores your profile, email, financial accounts, transactions, loans, investments, categories and preferences locally. With a configured backend and sign-in, financial records and portable preferences are sent to the operator's Supabase project. Passwords and email verification are handled by Supabase Auth. Cloud rows are restricted to the signed-in user by database policies.

Optional permissions

On Android, notification access lets MINARTH process bank and UPI alerts on-device, including in the background. If you separately enable Gmail payment notifications, available bank-payment notification previews are also processed; MINARTH does not access your mailbox. Recent extracted messages may be retained locally in notification history, and parsed payment information and source message text can be included in saved transactions and synced to your cloud account. You can turn off extraction in Settings or revoke notification access in Android Settings. MINARTH does not require SMS inbox or call-log permission.

Android may also retain an account's last four digits locally to suggest adding an unrecognized account. The separate account prompt does not automatically create an account or save a transaction.

If you enable location tagging, your device location can be attached to a transaction and synced with that record. Notification access and location tagging are off by default for new installations. Push notification permission controls payment prompts and budget alerts. Biometrics and passcode credentials remain device-local.

When location tagging resolves a place name, coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud's reverse-geocoding service and, if necessary, OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Those providers also receive network metadata such as your IP address. This happens even before you save a transaction; turning off location tagging prevents these location requests. Review BigDataCloud's privacy policy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

MINA: optional on-device AI assistant

If you choose to install the optional Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct model, MINARTH downloads its model files from Hugging Face. Hugging Face receives ordinary network metadata, such as your IP address, for that download. The model and runtime occupy about 533 MB in this device's browser/WebView storage. After installation, assistant prompts, temporary chat history, report content and report commentary are processed locally; MINARTH does not send them to Supabase, analytics or an AI service. Chat and report content are not saved by MINARTH and are discarded when the assistant closes. Exported report files are controlled by you.

Do not enter passwords, verification codes, account numbers or other private details into MINA. A local best-effort filter blocks some recognisable secrets and masks email addresses and long digit sequences before inference, retaining the last four digits. It cannot detect every name, secret or identifier. Transaction descriptions and category names may reach the local model for categorisation, but the merchant/person and note fields are not sent. The model receives temporary category aliases instead of stored category IDs. Report inference receives derived findings, not raw investment, demat or account records. The model has no database tools or direct access to authentication or account-aggregator consent data.

Financial figures in reports are computed by MINARTH's deterministic calculations. AI can select only findings derived from those results. Gold/silver answers, when available, use the locally cached IBJA benchmark already used by Investments and show its source and date; the assistant does not fetch live prices in response to a chat message. When enabled and the app is unlocked, local AI can categorise eligible uncategorised notification/import transactions and rule-categorised transactions. Uncertain suggestions and rule disagreements require review; unknown-origin existing transactions require your explicit request, and user-edited categories are not automatically overwritten. Cloud conflicts are not silently applied. Categories that you approve or that are automatically applied are financial records and continue to sync to your private Supabase account. Local AI does not mean the entire app works offline. Model files stay in browser/WebView cache storage, which is not an encrypted vault; Settings lets you disable AI and remove the downloaded model.

Other services and storage

Web hosting serves the app and Google Fonts supplies typefaces. These providers receive normal network metadata such as IP addresses. The current build does not offer paid subscriptions or real-time tracking. Receipt attachments, savings goals and manual broker snapshots remain on-device. Profile images you save are included in your cloud preferences. Exported backup files are under your control.

Controls and deletion

Use Settings to export records, change permissions, sign out, reset records or permanently delete your account. Cloud account deletion requires an internet connection and removes the associated live database rows after server confirmation. Copies on other offline devices, exported files and provider backups may persist separately. Local browser/WebView storage is not an encrypted vault; use your device screen lock.
