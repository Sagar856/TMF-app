Information used

MINARTH stores your profile, email, financial accounts, transactions, loans, investments, categories and preferences locally. With a configured backend and sign-in, financial records and portable preferences are sent to the operator's Supabase project. Passwords and email verification are handled by Supabase Auth. Cloud rows are restricted to the signed-in user by database policies.

Optional permissions

On Android, notification access lets MINARTH process bank and UPI alerts on-device, including in the background. Parsed payment information and source message text can be included in saved transactions and synced to your cloud account. You can turn off extraction in Settings or revoke notification access in Android Settings. MINARTH does not require SMS inbox or call-log permission.

If you enable location tagging, your device location can be attached to a transaction and synced with that record. Notification access and location tagging are off by default for new installations. Push notification permission controls payment prompts and budget alerts. Biometrics and passcode credentials remain device-local.

When location tagging resolves a place name, coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud's reverse-geocoding service and, if necessary, OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Those providers also receive network metadata such as your IP address. This happens even before you save a transaction; turning off location tagging prevents these location requests. Review BigDataCloud's privacy policy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Other services and storage

Web hosting serves the app and Google Fonts supplies typefaces. These providers receive normal network metadata such as IP addresses. The current build does not offer paid subscriptions or real-time tracking. Receipt attachments, savings goals and manual broker snapshots remain on-device. Profile images you save are included in your cloud preferences. Exported backup files are under your control.

Controls and deletion

Use Settings to export records, change permissions, sign out, reset records or permanently delete your account. Cloud account deletion removes the associated live database rows. Copies on other offline devices, exported files and provider backups may persist separately. Local browser/WebView storage is not an encrypted vault; use your device screen lock.
