Delete your MINARTH account

Open the web app, sign in with the account you want to delete, then choose Settings > Account > Delete account. Confirm permanent deletion. No Android installation is needed.

This deletes the sign-in account and associated cloud transactions, accounts, investments, loans, categories and preferences. Reset all data alone does not delete the sign-in account.

Deletion clears the current device's cached records. Other offline devices and exported backup files may still contain copies; clear their app data and remove exported files separately. Provider backups are subject to the operator's configured retention policy.
